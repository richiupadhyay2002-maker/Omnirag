The OmniRAG Sample Document
===========================

This file exists so you can try the evaluation harness without preparing your own
documents. Replace these entries with facts from your real files.

SECTION 1: Geography Facts
The capital of France is Paris. The longest river in the world is the Nile, at
approximately 6,650 kilometers. Mount Everest, on the border of Nepal and China,
is the tallest mountain on Earth at 8,849 meters.

SECTION 2: Space Facts
According to this document, the Perseverance Mars rover landed on the red planet
in February 2021. The James Webb Space Telescope launched in December 2021 and
began science operations in July 2022.

SECTION 3: About This Document
This sample document was written by the OmniRAG team to demonstrate the retrieval
evaluation pipeline. All facts above are self-contained: you can ask the golden
questions in golden_questions.csv and verify retrieval and answer quality.

SECTION 4: Cross-file links

more detail. The sample API file (sample_api.py) shows a cosine_similarity
